    Charts marked Simulated use supplementary data generated in the browser (seeded RNG, seed 42), because the CSV has no stock, due-date or payment fields. The order-lines filter does not apply to inventory charts.

    ⚠ Simulated data for demonstration PT Maju Distribusi Nusantara is a fictional client. No figure on this page is a real business result.

    • From CSV (10,000 simulated orders, AS_IS and TO_BE): processing time, P90, overselling, errors, invoice lead time, volumes, top customers.
    • Generated in browser (mulberry32, seed 42, reproducible): AR aging (invoice values and days past due), stock-accuracy cycle counts, stockout events.
    • Charts by Chart.js 4.4.1 (cdnjs). No other network requests; nothing is stored in the browser.
